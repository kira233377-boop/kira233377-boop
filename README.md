# kirzhang

**Fangfei Zhang** · Quantitative finance, statistics, and optimization

I am an M.S. student in **Computational Finance and Risk Management at the University of Washington**, with a B.Sc. in Applied Statistics and a minor in Economics from Western University.

My interests lie at the intersection of **empirical finance and decision-making under uncertainty**. I use statistical modeling, optimization, and simulation to study market behavior, portfolio decisions, and financial risk.

### Research interests

- **Empirical finance:** asset pricing, financial econometrics, leveraged and inverse ETFs, and market regimes.
- **Optimization and decision-making:** stochastic optimization, dynamic asset allocation, portfolio construction, and simulation.
- **Statistical learning:** financial time series, forecasting, and machine learning with careful out-of-sample evaluation.

### Selected research and academic projects

| Area | Work |
| --- | --- |
| Leveraged ETFs · Independent research | Estimating realized leverage and volatility decay across market regimes and holding horizons; implementing and validating estimators in R using simulated GBM paths. |
| Dynamic asset allocation · Graduate course project | Solving finite-horizon portfolio decisions with Bellman recursion and evaluating state-dependent policies through Monte Carlo simulation. |
| Pairs trading · Individual replication project | Replicating a classical equity pairs-trading strategy in Python, with factor regressions, placebo tests, block bootstrap, and sensitivity analysis. |
| Treasury yield forecasting · Individual course project | Comparing univariate and multivariate time-series models in a reproducible R Markdown workflow, with residual diagnostics and forecast evaluation. |
| Portfolio optimization · Graduate course projects | Applying linear programming to pension cash-flow matching and quadratic programming to REIT benchmark transitions. |
